## Guion (≈80 palabras)
1. This spot is in three countries at once.
2. But for almost a hundred years... it was in four.
3. And that fourth country doesn't exist anymore.
4. This is the Vaalserberg. The Netherlands, Belgium and Germany meet right here.
5. The fourth was Neutral Moresnet. A tiny territory nobody could agree on... because of a zinc mine.
6. Some even tried to turn it into an Esperanto state.
7. Then, in 1920... it was erased from the map.
8. Would you stand here? Save this for your Europe trip.
9. Next: the island that changes country every six months.
